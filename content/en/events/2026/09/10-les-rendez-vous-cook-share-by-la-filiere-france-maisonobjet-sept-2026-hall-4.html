11/09 to 15:15 : Made In France : Origin France Garantie, EPV and Geographical Indications

Made in France: trust labels at the service
companies, distributors and consumers How these approaches
do they create value for businesses, distributors and
consumers? Meeting expectations
of traceability, quality and sovereignty
the industrial sector, Origin France Garantie (OFG) , Enterprise
du Patrimoine Vivant (French Living Heritage) and Geographical Indications (GI)  are required as benchmarks of confidence. They value know-how
to strengthen the competitiveness of enterprises and respond to the
new consumer expectations.

But how
Do they work? How are they complementary? What benefits
Do they provide manufacturers, distributors and end customers?
How can they better tell the origin, quality and
the authenticity of French products?
A Round Table
the main players in these approaches of valorisation
decrypt their stakes and share concrete experiences.
Speakers

Tristan De Witte - President of Rivalen & President of
Réseau Excellence EPV

Beatrice Brandt - Director General of the French Jacquard
& Member of the Réseau excellence Gastronomy Committee

Gilles Attaf - President of Origin France Garantie

Audrey Aubard - Secretary General of FFIGIA

Thierry Villotte - President of the Confederation of the Arts

Made in France: trust labels at the service companies, distributors and consumers

How these approaches do they create value for businesses, distributors and consumers?Meeting expectations of traceability, quality and sovereignty the industrial sector, Origin France Garantie (OFG), Enterprise du Patrimoine Vivant (French Living Heritage) and Geographical Indications (GI) are required as benchmarks of confidence.

They value know-how to strengthen the competitiveness of enterprises and respond to the new consumer expectations. But how Do they work? How are they complementary? What benefits Do they provide manufacturers, distributors and end customers? How can they better tell the origin, quality and the authenticity of French products?

A Round Table the main players in these approaches of valorisation decrypt their stakes and share concrete experiences.

Speakers

  • Tristan De Witte - President of Rivalen & President of Réseau Excellence EPV
  • Beatrice Brandt - Director General of the French Jacquard & Member of the Réseau excellence Gastronomy Committee
  • Gilles Attaf - President of Origin France Garantie
  • Audrey Aubard - Secretary General of FFIGIA
  • Thierry Villotte - President of the Confederation of the Arts Table (CAT)
