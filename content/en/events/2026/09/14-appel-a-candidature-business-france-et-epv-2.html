Business France UK is now accepting applications for the 18th edition of the French Craft Collective, taking place on 12 May 2027 in London, as part of London Craft Week.

This leading event is dedicated to French craftsmanship and métiers d'art, giving selected companies the opportunity to meet, in a single day, key UK high-end industry specifiers, including interior architects, decorators, designers and specialist firms.
