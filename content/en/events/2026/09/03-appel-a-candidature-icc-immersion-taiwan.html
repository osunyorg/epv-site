ICC Immersion Taiwan 2027is an international support program dedicated to industries Cultural and Creative (ICC), carried byFrench Institute and Business France, in the framework of the France 2030 plan.

body: >-


This
Long immersion program aims to accompany French companies
innovative in their international development through preparation
in-depth marketing, targeted linkages and field immersion
Taiwan. This
edition is intended for companies in the following sectors:


Table Arts
Crafts
Decoration and interior design
Furniture
Jewellery and jewellery

Fashion and accessories

Why Taiwan?

Taiwan
is now one of the most dynamic markets in Asia, characterized by
with a strong purchasing power and a real appeal for design. The
Taiwanese consumers attach particular importance to know-how,
the history of the brands and the quality of the products, of which
benefit French companies from trades and the art of living. The
The programme will run from January to April 2027 and will include:


a preparatory phase with webinars and
individual appointments;
a 9-day collective immersion in Taiwan from 18 to
26 April 2027;
personalized support before, during and after
mission.
