Participate in our next meeting between Companies of the Heritage Vivant of Île-de-France, to be held within the Atelier Thiery, a house of excellence specialized in leaf gilding and the painted decoration since 1971.

body: >-


For more than 50 years, the Thiery Workshop
perpetuating rare craftsmanship, where the mastery of the gesture, the
precision of detail and the nobility of materials give life to decorations
of exception. Between tradition and creativity, gilding and painted decorations
sublime the surfaces and interiors, testifying to an artistic heritage
quality.
A privileged immersion in a universe where
gold, color and matter meet, revealing all the richness of
trades that contribute to the preservation and transmission of
French excellence.
