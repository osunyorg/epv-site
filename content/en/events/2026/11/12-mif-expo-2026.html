From 12 to 15 November, French know-how will be in the spotlight at the MIF Expo - The Salon du Made in France Hey! Stand F70

On that occasion, Enterprise and Discovery, Réseau Excellence EPV, the FFIGIA and SGS - Enterprise Label for Living Heritage (EPV) - Excellence in French know-how will join forces on a common stand, with a shared ambition:
To enhance the excellence of our companies
Discover their know-how
Strengthening the attractiveness of our territories
