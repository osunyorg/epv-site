body: >-

Perfect agreement between tradition and modernity

House
founded in 1942 and now managed by Vincent Morin-Desevedavy ,
grandson of the founder  Jules Desevedavy  and pianist
passionate, innovative company (digital pianos, keyboards and synthesizers)
in the field of electronics), while remaining attached to its roots, to the profession of "factor"
and the tradition of acoustic pianos.
With the love of music
blazed to the body, all the staff accomplish their mission with passion and
professionalism. Always giving way to listening. To guarantee the
customer satisfaction, whether a beginner, confirmed or professional pianist.

A unique shop in Europe!

Open
in 2016, and exclusively dedicated to pianos, keyboards and organs, this space
more than 1350 m2 has been elected « Best Piano Store in Europe » at the Frankfurt International Music Fair in April 2017. This
the quality of the choice outlined, the relevance of the Board, and
the technical expertise of the house.
