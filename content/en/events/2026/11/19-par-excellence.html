From November 19 to 21, 2026, the Artisans d'Excellence show returns to Paris for a third edition under the sign of the subject matter, the contemporary creation and the excellence of the art professions.

body: >-

Link Ticketing
https://artisansdexcellence.fr/


This must-see event of the Contemporary Decorative Arts will bring together some 100 artists, workshops and art manufacturers, including many Live Heritage Enterprises (Entreprise du Patrimoine Vivant - EPV ) . Wood, glass, metal, textile, earth or leather: exceptional know-how will be in the spotlight through unique creations, where the mastery of gesture meets innovation and contemporary creation. This edition will feature a new set design, designed by Brice Tual, as well as four spaces dedicated to know-how, luminaires, eco-responsible materials and the art of receiving.
