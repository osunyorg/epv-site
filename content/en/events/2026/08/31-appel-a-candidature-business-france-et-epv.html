Business France invites you to participate in the Exhibition French Art of Living in Brazil, to be held from 17-19 March 2027 in São Paulo, within the Casa Higienópolis, emblematic of the French-Brazilian heritage.

Thought as a showcase of French excellence, this exhibition will gather during three days the main Brazilian actors of luxury, design, decoration and architecture, offering French companies the opportunity to value their know-how with a visitorate professional and develop new business opportunities on the Brazilian market.

Head for Brazil: an exceptional showcase for French know-how


1st Latin American luxury market with 1.3
Million luxury consumers and growing demand
for products with history, authenticity and excellence.

São Paulo accounts for almost 75% of the total consumption of
luxury goods of the country ,
providing privileged access to the main prescribers, distributors,
architects, decorators, collectors and high-end customers.
Brazilian consumers attach importance
quality, manufacturing, customisation and
cultural value of products.
France enjoys an image in Brazil
particularly strong Article
of living, crafts of excellence, creation and heritage .

The agreement EU-Mercosur ,
implemented provisionally since 2026, is gradually improving
market access conditions for French companies.

Registration until 15 December 2026

Opportunities to meet with distributors,

This
