Business France is pleased to invite you to participate 17-21 February at the Art de Vivre exhibition in New Delhi, organised at the fair India Design ID, appointment High-end design reference in India. The aim of this operation is to promote the French know-how in the interior decoration, furniture, table arts and art objects to a qualified audience interior architects, decorators, importers, distributors and Indian prescribers.

body: >-

The offer includes:

Exhibition of your
products in a shared design space at India Design
ID;
Preparation Webinar
on the Indian market and local business practices;
Targeted promotion
architects, distributors, prescribers, concept stores and shops
multibrands;
Networking programme
with Indian design buyers and professionals (VIP preview, cocktails,
professional meetings, visits to workshops);
Participation in
French Savoir-Faire contest;
Collective communication
before and during the event (press relations, digital communication and
visibility with specialised media).

Why India?

With a habitat market estimated at €29 billion by 2029  and annual growth of 11,2 % , India is now one of
most dynamic markets in the world for furniture and decoration. The
demand is increasing particularly in premium segments, where brands
France has a strong recognition for their quality,
creativity and know-how.

Application before 30 October 2027

LINK REGISTRATION
https://extranet-btob.businessfrance.fr/expositionADV India2027/

Possible subsidies

50 % support
the participation cost for GEM members under the
CODIFAB Convention;
Potential for support
for companies in the Arts de la
table.
