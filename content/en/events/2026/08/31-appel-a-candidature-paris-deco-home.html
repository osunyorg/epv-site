the leading event of decoration in France, Paris Deco Home "Rive Gauche", opens for the first time its network to the EPV. An exhibition-sale will take place at the École d'Architecture Paris-Malaquais (Saint-Germain-des-Prés) in front of 61,000 visitors, 71% of whom are international.

Applications opened until 10 October 2026 with Romain Juilha.

body: >-


With 16 years of expertise and fame acquired with Paris Deco Off, Paris Deco Home "Rive Gauche" is the leading event in the decoration market in France. For its 16th edition, the rendez-vous of professionals is taking a new step: it opens its network for the first time ever to companies in the art professions.

Unpublished exhibition-sale dedicated to exceptional know-how

The art object, contemporary decorative arts and exceptional know-how are at the heart of the journey. This first exhibition-sale craft will take place within the École d'Architecture Paris-Malaquais , epicentre of the course surrounded by nearly 100 exhibitors distributed in the emblematic galleries of the district (rues de Seine, Callot, Mazarine, Les Beaux-Arts...).
In a classroom on the first floor of this Art Deco building, a selection of objects from the French decorative arts will take place in a new set: a Giant checker of 12 bases, deployed on the main plateau and mezzanine, for a total space of 150 m2. The works will be directly accessible to visitors, as close as possible to transformed materials and unique gestures.

An audience of international prescribers

Paris Deco Home is a 100% business journey that brought together in 2026 61,000 visitors , of which 71% are international (leading Americans, Italian and English agencies, Scandinavian decorators, Mediterranean visitors booming, growing presence in India, Asia and the Gulf). 94% of visitors are professional decorators: contracts, publishers, decorators, architectural agencies, public administrations and designers.

Two exposure formulae


Damier Pack - €6,000 tax excl.: a base (H70 x L100 x L100 cm), registration fee, signposting, police station and communication.

Mezzanine Pack - 7 500 € tax excl. : all shelf (8 m long), same services included.
The registration fee includes a double page in the digital catalogue, an online press sheet, a search engine on the site and a presence on social networks (over 80,700 Instagram followers, 194 press/TV/web releases and 1.1 million Instagram views in 2026).

Schedule of applications

Until 10 October 2026 : application to Romain Juilha ( romain.juilha@outlook.fr ), with history of the house, 5 visuals of works, technical characteristics/know-how and portrait of the leader.
Week of 12 October 2026 Selection committee and response to candidates.
