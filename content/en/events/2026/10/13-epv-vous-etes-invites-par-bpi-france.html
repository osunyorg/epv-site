Jérôme Bouquet, Director of the Ile-de-France Network at Bpifrance, Fabien Frigosi Regional Director of Paris at Bpifrance, Marie Lefèvre, International Delegate at Bpifrance, Léa Lisiecki Innovation Delegate at Bpifrance, Tristan De Witte, President of the Réseau Excellencce EPV and Emmanuelle Andrieux, President of the Réseau Excellence Ile-De-France.

have the pleasure of inviting the EPVs of the Réseau Excellence to an exclusive morning to discover how the Life heritage enterprises can build on solutions Bpifrance to finance, secure and accelerate their growth internationally.

Appointment: Tuesday, 13 October 2026 - from 9am to 1pm to Hub Bpifrance Haussmann, Paris

Event Program

Alongside the Réseau Excellence EPV , the French Touch  and Business France , we will present you concrete solutions to speed up your development projects, identify new markets and value your know-how internationally.



9.30 a.m. - 9:40 Introduction by Jérôme Bouquet, Director of the Île-de-France and Overseas Network of Bpifrance.


9.40 a.m. - 9:50 am  : The Réseau Excellence EPV: value and develop French know-how, by Tristan de Witte, President of the Réseau Excellence.


9.50 am - 10:00 a.m.  : Panorama of Entreprises du Patrimoine Vivant, by French Touch.


10:00 - 10:30 Innovation as a driver of growth and development: all financing solutions dedicated to EPV companies.


11:00 - 11:30  : Bpifrance devices dedicated to EPV companies abroad: how to export your know-how?

11.30 a.m. - 11:40  : Business France support for the international development of EPV companies.

11:50 - 12:10  : Cross-testimony of EPV companies.


12:10 - 1 p.m.  : Cocktail networking.
