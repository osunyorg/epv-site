From 12 to 15 November 2026, the EPV Réseau Excellence participated in the MIF Expo in Paris, alongside FFIGIA and SGS, Manager of the Enterprise du Patrimoine Vivant (EPV) label. The aim of this collective presence was to promote exceptional trades, to enhance the value of labeled companies and to contribute to the dissemination of French know-how.

body: >-

A collective reach for French excellence

Developed by Les Eastern Lyons , the common stand welcomed visitors throughout the duration of the show. The presence of Grégory Laurent, President of the Network Excellence EPV Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, demonstrated the commitment of regional representatives to the promotion of the EPV label and its companies. The showcase and demonstration workshops presented the diversity of trades and know-how represented in the Network. Visitors were able to discover the gestures and techniques of several companies, including The Bags of the Shepherd , Malongo , The Corvette , the Umbrellas of Cherbourg , Benoit the Artisan and Max Capdebarthes Workshops. This presence was also an opportunity to highlight companies with geographical indications, in particular Lartigue 1910 and Weaving , both labeled for their know-how in Basque laundry as well as the Corvette for its GI for Marseilles soaps.

Institutional exchanges to strengthen recognition of the EPV label

The visits of several public figures marked this edition, giving rise to exchanges on the issues of preservation and transmission of French know-how. On Thursday, Vincent Gaud, President of the Chamber of Trades and Crafts of Lyon, met Grégory Laurent. This visit served as a reminder of the partnership between CMA and the EPV Excellence Network, particularly in the areas of communication and awareness of exceptional trades. Grégory Laurent and Olivier Ducatillon also discussed with Natacha Polony the need to preserve and enhance the EPV label. Another highlight was the visit by Serge Papin, Minister for SMEs and Crafts. His attachment to the label was expressed by these words: "I love the EVPs!". On this occasion, a pine's EPV from the Manufacture des Fèves Colas, a company labeled Burgundy-Franche-Comté, was handed over to him. Finally, Yaël Braun-Pivet, President of the National Assembly, was met at the stand. The exchanges focused on the development of the living heritage enterprises. The discussions with Cécile Pierre also allowed us to discuss the issues of know-how tourism and business visit, notably in the framework of the Michelin know-how card, which values the EPV label. On Friday, Grégory Laurent met with Olivia Grégoire, whose parliamentary work focused on heritage and crafts. This meeting allowed us to continue exchanges around the recognition of trades of excellence and the preservation of know-how.

Highlights under the sign of sharing

The exhibitors' breakfast brought together participating companies around a moment of conviviality and exchange. The Moulin de la Veyssière, the Biscuiterie Jeannette, the Biscuitry of the Abbey , confectionery of high voges and Malongo contributed to this event through their donations. A raffle, with a coffee machine offered by Malongo, was also organized. Moreover, Leon Martin stationery offered EPV logotyped silk paper for participating companies. This initiative helps to increase the visibility of the label and enhance the products of labeled companies.

An intervention on MIF TV dedicated to the protection and enhancement of Made in France

On Saturday, November 14, from 1 p.m. to 1.40 p.m., the EPV Excellence Network participated in a panel discussion on MIF TV hosted by Anthony Vitorino on the theme "Protecting and valuing Made in France". The discussions focused on ways to better identify, value and protect French manufacturing, relying in particular on labels, certifications and indications of origin. The fight against Franco-washing and deceptive practices, the strengthening of customs controls and public awareness of industrial realities were also discussed. This intervention allowed us to recall the role of the Entreprise du Patrimoine Vivant label in the recognition of the know-how of excellence and to stress the importance of making French companies better known, particularly through the business visit and the discovery of the trades.

A collective dynamic to continue

Participation in the MIF Expo 2026 illustrated the importance of cooperation between companies, institutional partners and actors in the development of living heritage. The EPV Excellence Network thanks all the partners and companies involved for this edition, as well as the personalities and visitors who came to meet French know-how. This collective mobilisation helps to increase the visibility of the EVP label and to continue the action of the Network in favour of the recognition, transmission and development of exceptional professions.
