body: >-


Icon of La Croisette since 1929, Hotel Martinez today combines its Art Deco heritage with a contemporary vision of luxury. More than just a distinction, the title of "Palace" rewards the excellence of an institution whose historic character, unique know-how and outstanding quality of service place the address among the most prestigious in the world.
