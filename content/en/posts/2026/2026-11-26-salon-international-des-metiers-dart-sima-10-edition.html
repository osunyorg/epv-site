Over the years, the International Professions d'Art (SIMA) has established itself as an essential appointment dedicated to the creation, excellence and transmission of know-how in the region Hauts-de-France.

body: >-


For three days, nearly 180 artisans and
artists, coming from all over France and the international, gathered for
introduce the general public to the richness and diversity of crafts.
Demonstrations, exhibitions, parades and encounters took place during this event
of exception. A true showcase of know-how, the Excellence Network actively contributes to
the development of crafts and their transmission to all
public.
