On October 13, 2026, Bpifrance, in connection with the Excellence Network EPV and the Réseau Excellence EPV Île-de-France, brought together key players in innovation and export on its premises. The aim was to give Heritage Enterprises Living the levers necessary for their development. Financing, innovation, international support, sharing of experiences: the morning involved exchanges and concrete solutions to spread French know-how.

A highlight: the signing of the convention with the CCEs

This morning was marked by the signing of a partnership agreement between the Réseau Excellence EPV and the Conseils du Commerce Exterior de la France (CCE). The agreement was signed by Tristan De Witte, President of the Réseau Excellence , and Sophie Sidos-Vicat, President of the CCE, in the presence of their leadership teams.
It reinforces a common ambition: to support the 1,300 companies labeled EPV in their conquest of international markets, mobilizing the expertise and resources of Team France Export.

CCEs, a network serving international

In all regions of France and in more than 150 countries, the EACs have been a network of 4,600 entrepreneurs and international experts for over a century. Appointed for three years by decree of the Prime Minister, they volunteered to the public authorities around four missions: advising the public authorities, supporting businesses, training young people internationally and promoting the attractiveness of France.
What the Convention provides for


International support for EPV companies , notably through mentoring provided by CCE experts from sectors or geographical areas

Cross promotion of the EPV label and the CCE network members of each association, with communications operations
Collaboration between regional association

Cross reflection on individual interests.
