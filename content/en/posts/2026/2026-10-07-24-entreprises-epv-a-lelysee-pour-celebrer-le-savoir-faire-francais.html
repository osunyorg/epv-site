The Great Exhibition of Made in France returns to the Elysée les 21 and 22 November 2026.

For this sixth edition, 104 products, each representing a French department or territory, were selected to highlight companies that produce, innovate and create value in the territories.

Among this selection, the Enterprises of the Heritage Living (EPV) occupy a special place. Furniture, table arts, gastronomy, textiles, crafts or industry: these companies illustrate the richness of French know-how. 24 companies and know-how represented
