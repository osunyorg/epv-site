Tax credits and support available: CIMA, CIR and grants for Living Heritage Enterprises On September 25, 2026, the Réseau Excellence EPV Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes and the Chambre de Métiers et de l'Artisanat Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes brought together the companies labeled for a webinar dedicated to financing excellence.

On the programme: a panorama of the devices, a zoom on the art trades tax credit (CIMA) presented by the DRFIP and the Tax Accompaniment of SMEs (AFPME) of Lyon, a focus on the research tax credit (CIR/CII) with Leyton, and the testimonies of two executives EPV: Sylvain Quidant (Cogitech) and Odile Dhavernas (Manufacture des Matis de Bourgogne).
