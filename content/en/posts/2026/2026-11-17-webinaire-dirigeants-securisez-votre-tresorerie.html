The Excellence Grand Est Network, in partnership with RYDGE Conseil, gives you an appointment for a webinar dedicated to a key issue for managers: securing the cash flow of their business.

body: >-


In a sometimes uncertain economic environment, identifying the first signals of financial fragility and acting at the right time is essential to anticipate difficulties and preserve the sustainability of its activity. Through this exchange time, two specialists in financial support for firms in difficulty will share their expertise and advice . Anne Lattore : Partner at RYDGE Conseil, Lead National Restructuring & Prevention of Difficulties. Ceylia Boegli : Manager Consulting at RYDGE Conseil. This webinar will address, among other things, the weak signals to be monitored, the means to anticipate financial difficulties and the levers of action to be mobilised to better secure its cash flow. Practical information: Date: Tuesday, December 15, 2026 Schedule : 1 p.m. - 2:00 pm Format: videoconference Participation: compulsory registration. To participate: please confirm your presence by email at contact@epvgrandest.com The connection link will be communicated to you following your registration. We expect many of you for this event dedicated to the business leaders of the network!
