On the occasion of the Premiere Classe show, at the beginning of the month, WSN highlighted the companies labeled Enterprise du Patrimoine Vivant (EPV) in the heart of the Jardin des Tuileries in Paris.

Through Épicerie des EPV, a selection of exceptional French houses was presented to visitors, offering a beautiful showcase to companies in our network.

body: >-

A selection of French houses of exception

The EPV grocery store brought together iconic companies, recognized for the quality of their products, the richness of their traditions and their commitment to preserving French know-how. Salty side , visitors were able to discover La Boutique du Gourmet - Georges Bruck, Groix & Nature, La Conserverie Sétoise since 1963 with the ranges Le Gourmand and La Belle des Mers by Azaïs-Polito, Giraudet and Alpina Savoie. Sweet side , Fouquet, Mulot & Petitjean, Confiseries des Hautes Vosges, Les Confitures du Cliont, La Trinitaine and Kubli have illustrated the richness and diversity of French confectionery, biscuits and gourmet specialities. The craftsmanship was also represented by Filt 1860, emblematic house of the French net, also present at the Premiere Classe show. This selection has led to a dialogue between gastronomy, crafts and contemporary creation around the same ambition: to make known companies that preserve exceptional gestures and techniques while making their jobs live.

A good visibility for companies labeled EPV

This initiative has made it possible to value the companies labeled EPV of our network in a prestigious setting, at the crossroads of fashion, creation and craftsmanship of excellence. The "Entreprise du Patrimoine Vivant" label distinguishes from French companies with crafts and industrial know-how of excellence. Their presence at Premiere Classe has helped to make the public discover the richness of a living heritage, carried by companies committed to transmission and innovation.

A valuable partnership with WSN

We extend our sincere thanks to WSN , which offered this visibility to EPV companies in our network at the Premiere Classe show. We particularly thank Frédéric Maus, Sylvie Pourrat, Karine Leclercq Margraff and the entire team for their involvement and contribution to the realization of this beautiful initiative. This collaboration illustrates the importance of partnerships in bringing French know-how to light, enhancing the visibility of labeled companies and fostering encounters between the worlds of fashion, gastronomy and crafts.
