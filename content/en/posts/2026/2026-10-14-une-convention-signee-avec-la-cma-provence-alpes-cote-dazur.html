On Monday, August 31, 2026, the Chambre des Métiers et de l'Artisanat de a Région Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur and the Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur & Corsica Excellence Network take a decisive step in signing a strategic partnership.

body: >-


"To establish such a partnership with the Chambre des Métiers et de l'Artisanat Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur is an important step for the Association Régionale EPV and the companies that join it. Working together to promote this state label, to unite and support together the registered companies are essential axes to meet the economic challenges we face (transmission, development in France and abroad, employment development, etc.). »
Guillaume Fievet
President of the Excellence Network EPV Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur and Corsica

The objective: to propel the exceptional know-how of our territories.

A very concrete plan of action for the regional economy:
Identify companies with rare, ancestral or high-tech know-how.
Personally accompany the preparation of applications for the state label EPV.
Valorize local nuggets on trade shows and increase their visibility.
Transmission, growth, employment, development in France and export... Faced with the economic challenges of our companies, this rapprochement offers artisans and companies concrete levers to transform their excellence into a powerful engine of development. This agreement is a strong commitment to Yannick Mazette , President of CMA Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur and Guillaume FIEVET, President of the Network Excellence EPV PACA & Corse in favor of living heritage and our regional talents. The signature was made in the presence of 3 companies labeled: Serge Bruna de la Savonnerie Marseillaise de la Licorne, Yann Gaudin de la Licorne Iron and steel Gaudin and Thibault Gignoux des Calissons Leonard Parli but also Stephan Figuiere , President of the Departmental Level Chamber of the Alpes-de-Haute-Provence and Roland Rolfo , President of the Departmental Level Chamber of Var.
