On 21 and 22 November 2026, the Palais de l'Élysée will host the 6th edition of the Grande Exposition du Made in France.

Among the 104 winners selected, 24 Enterprises du Patrimoine Vivant (EPV) will highlight the richness, diversity and excellence of French know-how.

body: >-

24 companies, the same ambition: making French know-how shine

A real showcase of French production, the Grande Exposition du Manufacture in France values companies that contribute every day to the economic and artisanal dynamism of our territories. For this sixth edition, 24 companies labeled Companies of the Heritage Vivant were selected from among the 104 winners. From various sectors of activity, they embody the vitality of French economic heritage, combining tradition, innovation, technical mastery and commitment to national manufacturing. Furniture, table arts, cutlery, textiles, gastronomy, musical instruments and construction materials: this selection reflects the breadth of know-how that makes our French companies singular and famous.

The EPV, ambassadors of French excellence

Beyond the diversity of the products presented, this selection demonstrates a common commitment: to preserve rare know-how, transfer skills, innovate and maintain quality production at the heart of the territories. The label Living Heritage Company (EPV) distinguishes French companies from crafts and industrial know-how of excellence. It values their technical mastery, their economic heritage and their ability to sustain sometimes unique trades and skills. The presence of 24 EPV companies among the winners of the Grande Exposition du Manufacture in France underlines the essential role of these companies in the preservation and renewal of the French productive heritage. On 21 and 22 November 2026, the Palais de l'Elysée will offer a privileged opportunity to discover these products and the know-how that make them possible. This exhibition highlights companies that each in their own way contribute to the influence of French manufacturing and the economic vitality of our regions. T Our congratulations to the 24 selected EPV companies! The Grande Exposition du Made in France is a great showcase for these companies that bring French excellence to life every day.
