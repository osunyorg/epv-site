Bertrand Gaume, prefect of the Hauts-de-France region, prefect of the North, awarded the diplomas to the newly labeled companies "Entreprise du Patrimoine Vivant" (EPV).

This national label distinguishes artisanal and industrial know-how of excellence, inherited from tradition and oriented towards the future.

The Réseau Excellence EPV Hauts-de-France, chaired by Bruno Toulemonde and accompanied by Claire Bricout, was present to congratulate its members and new labeled members.

15 Hauts-de-France companies join this network of excellence:
  🔹 Ateliers Courageux
🔹 Ets J. Toulemonde Père et Fils
🔹 Ets Pierre Duriez
🔹 Faïence Ponchon
🔹 Fonderies de Sougland
🔹 Herbeau
🔹 Lemaitre Demeestere
🔹 Les Tissages de la Lys SA
🔹 Métal Finitions
🔹 Moulinage et retorderie de l'Oise (MERO)
🔹 Parqueterie de la Lys
🔹 Potencier Broderies Sarl
🔹 Tétard Haudiquez Grisoni
🔹 Tissage d'Art de Lys
🔹 Cornilleau SAS  

Congratulations to these companies that wear the colors of Hauts-de-France and French excellence.

We would also like to thank SGS, the manager of the EPV label for his support and presence at this event.
