27 Living Heritage Enterprises Winners

27 Living Heritage Enterprises were selected as winners: a pride for all the Réseau Excellence, which represents on a daily basis their exceptional know-how : François Pouenat  •  La Rochère  •  Le Soulor 1925  •  Les Poteries d'Albi - Fait Main France 🇫🇷 depuis 1891, entreprise du patrimoine vivant.  • Fayol •  Aprotec - Engine technologies  •  Fontanille Scop  •  PEUGEOT SAVEURS SNC  •  Vuillet Vega  •  Les Ateliers de Gautier  •  SOCIETE RIVALIN  •  La Trinitaine  •  LES PETITES MARIES  •  MANUFACTURE TISMAIL  •  Nicolas SOUCHET  • New Live Mobility •  MANUFACTURE PAPIERS PEINTS ZUBER ET CIE  •  PEIGNAGE DUMORTIER  •  MP VITRAGE  •  Le Parapluie de Cherbourg  • empreinte •  Ciergerie de Lourdes  • Etablissements Cambres •  LAMBERT MANUFIL INDUSTRIES

We are pleased to have been fully associated with this edition:

Tristan De Witte , President of Réseau Excellence, had the honour to participate in the 2025 jury and to speak at the round table moderated by Anthony Vitorino to talk about "The importance of buying manufactured in France?", alongside Nathalie Leroy (CNA) and Tristan Meneret (In France). An essential exchange to recall the economic, social and territorial impact of French buying, and to value those who produce here, everywhere in our regions. Philippe Neymarc, Secretary-General of Reseau Excellence  Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes also participated in the round table: "GEFEF 2025 - behind the scenes of the project", with the organizing teams of Elysée, DGDDI and Enterprise Directorate-General - Charles de Bisschop . A rare opportunity to highlight women and men who work for months to bring this great national event to life.
