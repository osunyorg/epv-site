At the foot of the Canigó, in Prades, La Manufacture Henri Privat is a must-have stage that brings to life an emblematic know-how of the Pyrénées-Orientales: the art of the Catalan Grenat.

House apart in the circle of Catalan jewellery, the only labeled Living Heritage Company (EPV), It combines tradition, demand and innovation. A family craft adventure initiated in 1945 carried today by the third generation, Henri Privat, Best Worker in France. All year round thousands of visitors attend its guided tours.
