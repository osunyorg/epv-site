De Buyer is a historic French manufacturer located in Val-d'Ajol in the Vosges region, specializing in the design and production of culinary articles. It boasts a bicentennial expertise (since 1830) forged around the working and transformation of metals (sheet steel, stainless steel, copper, aluminum) in accordance with French manufacturing traditions. Recognized with the Entreprise du Patrimoine Vivant (Living Heritage Company) label, the company relies on a team of exceptional craftsmen and workers, ensuring rigorous work to design robust, precise, and durable equipment.

Main activities

  • Professional cooking equipment: frying pans (notably the iconic carbon steel pans that require seasoning), saucepans, sauté pans, stewpots, and woks, available in noble metals (copper, stainless steel, carbon steel) for all types of cooking
  • Pastry equipment: various molds (non-stick steel, stainless steel, silicone), tart rings, entremet frames, baking mats, and technical utensils such as "Le Tube" pressure pastry syringe and dough sheeters
  • Preparation and cutting utensils: high-precision mandolines, vegetable slicers, chef's knives, spatulas, whisks, and mixing bowls
  • Mills and seasoning: manufacture of pepper, salt, nutmeg, and spice mills, combining high-quality mechanisms with durable materials like wood
  • Innovation and contemporary collections: development of modular ranges (LOQY collection with removable handles and grips), outdoor cooking equipment, and manufacturing committed to an eco-responsible and sustainable approach
