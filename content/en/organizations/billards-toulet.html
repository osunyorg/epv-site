Founded in 1857, Maison Toulet is a French company specializing in the design, manufacture, and sale of high-end game tables.

At Maison Toulet, we design exceptional games—pool tables, foosball tables, and other game tables—that blend design, craftsmanship, and emotion. But our craft goes beyond manufacturing: we create durable pieces, designed to stand the test of time, preserve memories, and embody the art of fine play.

Every Toulet piece is a distillation of expertise, high standards, and the passing down of tradition. Because for us, a game table is not just a piece of furniture: it is a future legacy.

Combining artisanal expertise with contemporary innovation, Maison Toulet has established itself as an essential reference in the world of games, both in France and internationally. Each product is designed and crafted in our workshops in Bondues, in the heart of the Hauts-de-France region, where we pay meticulous attention to materials, finishes, and aesthetics.
There, we work by hand to create custom pieces using wood, metal, paint, varnish, and leather, ensuring that every piece offers exceptional quality and a unique level of customization.

From traditional billiard tables to designer models—convertible into dining tables or multi-game versions—as well as custom-made foosball tables with endless customization options, our creations meet the expectations of private clients, architects, interior designers, and professionals alike.

Toulet isn’t just about billiards: we can also design custom games tailored to our customers’ preferences—poker tables, game tables, pinball machines, dartboards, chess sets, backgammon sets, and much more. We also offer customized layout solutions to create unique gaming spaces, whether for private rooms, clubs, or commercial settings.

Driven by a commitment to excellence, customization, and a passion for gaming, Maison Toulet continues to innovate, offering its customers products that are as aesthetically pleasing as they are functional—designed to stand the test of time with elegance.
