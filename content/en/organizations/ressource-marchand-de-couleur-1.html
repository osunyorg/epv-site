A French manufacturer of paints and wallpapers since 1946, Resource is an independent family home, heir to an artisanal know-how which consisted of exploiting the natural matter of the quarries d ́ocres of Roussillon in Provence from the late 1940s.

Resource has always had as an engine the desire to undertake sustainable. The place of ecology is at the heart of its development, all the paintings and wallpapers, without exception, are made in France in the workshops of the Maison with a preference for reasoned manufacturing. The Resource spirit is illustrated by an amazing creativity associated with a permanent innovation capacity. Each of the shades is designed by its internal design studio to offer an exceptional grade quality.
