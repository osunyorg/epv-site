The CARLHIAN Ets are also present on the 5 continents thanks to the quality of their metal stripes and ribbons made by their subsidiary iseroise, USINE GALLIA specialized in narrow weaving mainly on shuttle trades (mechanical Jacquard) but also on needles.

With more than 8,000 drawings, these stripes are intended for the ornamentation of liturgical, military, apparate, folk, professional (air and maritime companies), as well as for historical reconstruction, protocol articles, decoration.
