Located in Dieuze, Escaliers Somme designs, manufactures, and installs custom staircases for residential and commercial clients. From workshop creation to the final touches, the company manages projects in their entirety throughout the Lorraine region (Meurthe-et-Moselle, Meuse, Moselle, Vosges) and Luxembourg.Recognized by the Treppenmeister network-inventor of the suspended staircase and European leader-the company combines cutting-edge technology with artisanal craftsmanship.

It works with high-quality materials (wood, steel, glass, stainless steel) to craft unique pieces tailored for both indoor and outdoor spaces.

Main activities

  • 100% custom manufacturing: Design of varied structures (suspended, floating, spiral, central stringer, quarter-turn) that adapt to the architecture of each space.
  • Creation of thematic ranges:
  • Industrial spirit (Loft & Ferro ranges): sleek design combining wood and metal.
  • Contemporary style (Verre & Senzu ranges): focus on luminosity and natural materials.
  • Timeless lines (Espace & Traditionnels ranges): classic models focusing on simplicity and elegance.
  • Customer support: A dedicated showroom in Dieuze (since 2005) allows clients to physically discover the models, materials, and finishes.
