For more than two centuries, Maison Pichon Uzès has perpetuated exceptional know-how in the heart of southern France.

In our workshop, each piece is shaped and enamelled by hand, revealing the precision of the gesture, the richness of the material and the depth of the hues.

Between heritage and contemporary creation, our collections reflect a sincere and refined art of living. The braiding embodies one of the most emblematic signatures of the House. Made from carefully assembled clay cords, our iconic baskets show rare control, where precision and balance give rise to both sculptural and delicate forms.

The richness of the colour palette is the other pillar of our identity. Intense, bright and subtly nuanced, the hues reveal matter in all its light.
