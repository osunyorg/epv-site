Alsace Lait is an independent dairy cooperative located in Hoerdt in the Bas-Rhin region, specializing in the processing of raw cow's milk. It draws on historical expertise, inherited from the Laiterie Centrale de Strasbourg founded in 1915, built around promoting the local terroir and respecting local traditions.

The team consists of approximately 260 employees and relies on a network of nearly 200 dairy farmers, guaranteeing gourmet, high-quality products from a committed agricultural supply chain (GMO-free feed and respect for animal welfare).

Main activities


  • Milks and creams: packaging of fresh milk collected nearby, and production of the iconic Alsace crème fraîche (awarded at the Concours Général Agricole and Label Rouge certified).
  • Fromage blanc and local specialties: production of the traditional plain Bibeleskaes and sweet, creamy fromages blancs for everyday enjoyment.
  • Yogurts and gourmet desserts: creation of 100% Alsace fruit yogurts, vanilla yogurts, Skyr (a Nordic recipe with an Alsatian heart), and various dairy desserts.
  • Fresh spreadable cheeses: creation of cheeses with a smooth and melting texture, made from crème fraîche for toasts, sandwiches, or appetizers.
  • Specific ranges and professional solutions: "MICHA & KIDS" range dedicated to children (free from artificial flavors, colors, or preservatives), and products intended for catering professionals (e.g., ready-to-use topping for tarte flambée)
