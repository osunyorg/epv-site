Brushing is an art that Altesse Studio has been practicing with elegance and passion since 1875 in its French workshops in Picardie.

Almost 150 years later, and after manufacturing several million brushes, our craftsman's house is proud to offer the best hairbrushes in the world.

The brush of a lifetime. The one that matches your needs.

Whether for detangling or caring to your hair: Altesse Studio's expertise is at the service of every hair's beauty.
