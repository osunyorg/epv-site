Founded in 1955 in La Trinity-sur-Mer by Lucien Petit, La Trinitaine has been perpetuating for over 70 years Breton biscuits know-how. Within its unique manufacturing workshop located in Saint-Philibert, in the Morbihan, Maison La Trinitaine develops its traditional biscuits, including its essential cakes and pure butter pallets, and its cigarette emblematic.

Inheritance of a gourmet tradition, she strives to preserve gestures, recipes and quality requirement that make its biscuits singular. Its know-how is based on the mastery of the manufacturing stages, careful selection of ingredients and a constant search for regularity and Gourmet. Faithful to its roots, La Trinitaine brings to life the Breton biscuit heritage while changing its recipes and creations as you want.

Through her cookies and her shopping network, she shares a strong idea of Brittany: generous, authentic and attached to the pleasure of meeting again.

The recognition Company of the Living Heritage testifies to its commitment to preserve and transmit French expertise of excellence, rooted in its territory for several generations.
