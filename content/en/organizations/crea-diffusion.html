Voici une traduction professionnelle et naturelle, adaptée à une présentation d'entreprise en anglais.Founded in 1988 in the Moselle region of France, CREA DIFFUSION is a family-owned company specializing in the fabrication of Solid Surface materials, particularly Corian®.For more than 35 years, the company has been putting its expertise at the service of architects, designers, shopfitters and manufacturers, bringing unique and ambitious projects to life. Over the years, CREA DIFFUSION has developed extensive know-how in the production of bespoke components and custom interior solutions.Its team supports every project from technical design through to final installation, combining skilled craftsmanship, innovation and advanced manufacturing capabilities.Based in Solgne, France, CREA DIFFUSION operates across a wide range of sectors, including retail, hospitality, public spaces, offices and luxury environments.Thanks to the versatility of the materials it transforms, the company manufactures everything from bespoke furniture, reception desks and architectural façades to custom interior fittings.

Recognized for the quality of its work and the excellence of its craftsmanship, CREA DIFFUSION was awarded the Entreprise du Patrimoine Vivant (EPV) label (Living Heritage Company), a prestigious French distinction honoring companies with exceptional artisanal and industrial expertise.Committed to innovation, quality and close customer relationships, CREA DIFFUSION continues to grow by supporting clients in the realization of increasingly ambitious projects, both in France and internationally.

Main Activities

  • Custom interior fit-outs for commercial spaces, luxury retail, hospitality and museums, including wall cladding, display plinths, bespoke furniture and reception counters
  • Architectural façades: design, manufacture and installation of large-format Corian® exterior cladding systems that are lightweight, durable and weather resistant
  • Industrial and medical production: serial manufacturing, including the C-Line® worktop solution, designed to meet the strict hygiene standards required for hospitals, laboratories and public facilities
  • Residential solutions: complete bathroom renovation systems through the Créa Bain range (vanity tops, shower trays and wall cladding), as well as the distribution of premium bathroom furniture
